# Competitor landscape: doctor booking / telehealth / patient-record platforms vs MediBridge (state as of October 2026)

Reliability note for the report writer: research was done 2026-10-04 with ~25 web calls. Three pages were fetched and read in full (Practo "About" page, Digital Health News article on Practo of 30 Jan 2026, HMPI article on ABDM of 9 Jul 2026). Every other cited finding comes from search-engine result summaries of the linked page, not from a full read of the page, so exact wording and figures should be treated as "as reported in search snippet". Where the snippet did not make clear which of several result URLs carried a figure, this is flagged. Feature claims that I could not source are kept out of "Cited Findings" and placed under "Inferences" or "Gaps".

## 1. What do the major platforms offer, feature by feature?

### Takeaway
Doctor discovery, slot booking, teleconsultation, digital prescriptions, stored health records, reviews and reminders are standard across the Indian majors (Practo, Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg, MediBuddy, Bajaj Finserv Health, Eka Care, HealthPlix) and the global ones (Zocdoc, Doctolib, Teladoc, MyChart, NHS App, Halodoc). Several of the names on the list are no longer independent (MFine, Lybrate, DocsApp, Babylon).

### Inferences
- Three of the ten Indian names in the brief are not live independent competitors in 2026: DocsApp is folded into MediBuddy, Lybrate belongs to Pristyn Care, MFine became part of LifeWell. A report should list them as historical.
- The Indian market splits into patient-facing marketplaces (Practo, Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg, MediBuddy, Bajaj) and doctor-facing EMR tools with a companion patient app (Eka Care, HealthPlix). MediBridge sits across both, like Practo (marketplace + Ray) and Eka (PHR + Eka Doc).
- Commercial platforms bundle pharmacy and lab commerce, which MediBridge does not have; MediBridge has no feature in the booking/records area that the majors lack as a category.

### Gaps
- DocOn: no usable source found in this session; nothing can be stated about its current features or status.
- athenaPatient and Healow: not searched; no findings.
- Teladoc feature list and Doctor Anywhere / Halodoc feature detail were not retrieved beyond one-line descriptions.
- Official first-party feature pages were not read for Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg, MediBuddy, HealthPlix, Zocdoc, Doctolib, MyChart; the feature claims above are from app-store listings and third-party write-ups.
- No per-platform confirmation of a clinic map, filter by consultation mode, or reschedule flow was retrieved, although these are widely understood to be standard.

## 2. What does India's government stack already provide (ABDM / ABHA / eSanjeevani / Scan and Share / consent manager)?

### Takeaway
The government already runs a national health ID, a personal-health-record layer, a free telemedicine service and QR-based OPD registration, at a scale of hundreds of millions. Its documented weak point is actual use of the records for continuity of care.

### Cited Findings
- ABHA is a 14-digit unique health identifier under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, intended as a portable, paperless, consent-based health record system that lets citizens access and share medical history across ABDM-enabled facilities. — [Drishti IAS, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/ayushman-bharat-health-accounts-abha/print_manually)
- As of July 2026: over 93 crore ABHA accounts and over 104 crore health records linked. — [PIB document, Jul 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jul/doc202676912801.pdf); [India Med Today (90 crore milestone)](https://indiamedtoday.com/ayushman-bharat-digital-mission-crosses-90-crore-abha-accounts-milestone/)
- ABHA account growth: 147 million (2021), 304 million (2022), 506 million (2023), 722 million (2024), 845 million (2025), above 900 million in 2026. — [OpenGov Asia](https://opengovasia.com/indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission-surpasses-900-million-abha-health-accounts/)
- ABDM registries: 500,000+ registered health facilities and 860,000+ enrolled healthcare professionals; 880+ million ABHA accounts at the time of writing, with the caveat that "the true denominator of unique active patients is materially lower than headline counts imply" because of CoWIN seeding. — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- Scan and Share: QR-code OPD registration launched October 2022; patient scans the QR at the registration counter with a PHR app and shares demographics; queue time reduced from 30-40 minutes to 5-10 minutes. As of November 2024: 6.64 crore tokens at 17,481 facilities across 35 states/UTs, 3.3 crore person-hours saved. Earlier milestones: 1 crore, 2 crore, 3 crore tokens. — [MoHFW press release](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/press-info/7908); [News On Air (3 crore)](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/national-health-authority-achieves-milestone-with-over-3-crore-opd-tokens-generated-through-abha-based-scan-and-share-service); [Drishti IAS, 10 Jun 2024](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/abha-based-scan-and-share-service/print_manually)
- Scan and Pay: patients scan a facility QR with a PHR app to view and pay open orders linked to their ABHA address. — [MoHFW press release](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/press-info/7908)
- eSanjeevani: over 48.95 crore consultations through 19,257 hubs and 141,341 spokes as of July 2026 (secondary source); 34 crore+ as on 2 Feb 2025 (Rajya Sabha answer); Karnataka alone over 3.09 crore. — [Omnicuris, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.omnicuris.com/medshots/panorama/esanjeevani-telemedicine-karnataka-uptake-clinical-insights_27-Aug-26_12-08); [Rajya Sabha PQ 267, 11 Feb 2025](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/758635/1/PQ_267_11022025_S79_p34_p42.pdf)
- Consent flow: when a provider wants records from another facility, the patient must find a notification in their PHR app, review time-bound consent parameters and approve the request. — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- Private PHR apps such as Eka Care are ABDM-approved and can create ABHA and pull records from ABDM-affiliated providers. — [App Store: Eka](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1561621558)

### Inferences
- "Patient views their records and prescriptions in one place" is the stated purpose of a national programme with 93 crore accounts. A closed, single-database student project cannot claim record portability as novel; it is strictly weaker than ABDM because its records do not leave the MediBridge database.
- The two ABHA totals (880+ million in the HMPI article, 93 crore in the PIB document) are consistent with different cut-off dates in mid-2026.

### Gaps
- abdm.gov.in and the eSanjeevani dashboard were not read directly; current October 2026 dashboard values are not confirmed. The eSanjeevani July 2026 figure rests on a secondary medical news site.
- Scan and Share token count after November 2024 was not found.
- No source retrieved on Unified Health Interface booking volumes, or on the ABHA app's own feature list (whether it has medication reminders or any timeline view).
- The MoHFW press-info URL is the most likely carrier of the November 2024 Scan and Share figures among the search results, but the snippet did not tie the figure to one URL unambiguously.

## 3. For each MediBridge feature, which platforms already provide it, and is any feature provided by none?

### Takeaway
Every MediBridge feature category has a sourced commercial or government equivalent. The only element for which no direct equivalent turned up is the specific patient-facing composite "Care Score" (adherence 40% / checkup recency 35% / follow-up completion 25%); its ingredients all exist separately, and this search was not exhaustive enough to call it unique.

### Cited Findings
- Doctor search / directory with verified profiles: Practo. — [Practo About](https://www.practo.com/company/about)
- Online appointment booking: Practo (9,000+ hospitals/clinics with online booking), Zocdoc (instant booking), Bajaj Finserv Health, Doctolib. — [Practo About](https://www.practo.com/company/about); [iDataLabs: Zocdoc](https://idatalabs.com/tech/products/zocdoc); [App Store: Bajaj Health](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id1487452389)
- Doctor-written digital prescriptions and EMR: Practo Ray, Eka Doc, HealthPlix. — [Practo About](https://www.practo.com/company/about); [App Store: Eka Doc](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1598859349); [HackMD comparison](https://hackmd.io/@wondrx/HkhssLzKGx)
- Patient view of records and prescriptions: Eka Care, Bajaj Finserv Health, ABHA-linked PHR apps, MyChart. — [App Store: Eka](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1561621558); [App Store: Bajaj Health](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id1487452389)
- Medication reminders and adherence tracking: Eka Care medication tracker; MyChart medication reminders and Care Companion; Medisafe (4 million+ users at the time of the cited release), which reports medication-level "adherence scores" computed by a proprietary algorithm from self-reported adherence and persistence, and reported 81% adherence among early users. — [Techjockey: Eka Care](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/eka-care); [Surety Systems](https://www.suretysystems.com/insights/how-can-epic-care-companion-improve-patient-health/); [Medisafe Adherence Grader](https://www.medisafe.com/medisafe-launches-free-adherence-grader-tool-that-ranks-adherence-to-pharma-products-worldwide/)
- Follow-up and preventive-care reminders: Zocdoc (preventive check-up reminders), MyChart (health-maintenance screening reminders), Eka Care and HealthPlix (automated follow-up reminders over SMS/WhatsApp). — [iDataLabs: Zocdoc](https://idatalabs.com/tech/products/zocdoc); [Premier Health](https://www.premierhealth.com/news-and-events/news/2013/01/29/dr-davis-shares-how-mychart-electronic-medical-records-play-a-role-in-preventive-care); [Techjockey: Eka Care](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/eka-care)
- Health timeline: Practo's app is described as offering a "medical timeline" of uploaded reports and prescriptions (third-party description). — [Apptunix](https://www.apptunix.com/blog/practo-business-model-and-revenue-model/)
- Doctor reviews: Practo (patient feedback), Zocdoc (reviews from real patients). — [AppMaster](https://appmaster.io/blog/how-to-make-an-app-like-practo); [iDataLabs: Zocdoc](https://idatalabs.com/tech/products/zocdoc)
- Notifications: NHS App push appointment reminders. — [Digital Health, Apr 2026](https://www.digitalhealth.net/2026/04/patients-across-england-can-now-check-appointments-via-nhs-app/)
- Health education content: MyChart Care Companion includes educational content inside care plans. — [Surety Systems](https://www.suretysystems.com/insights/how-can-epic-care-companion-improve-patient-health/)
- Doctor verification (the admin function): Practo verification is described as covering medical licence, clinic registration and identity. — [AppMaster](https://appmaster.io/blog/how-to-make-an-app-like-practo)
- Role-based admin dashboards, doctor management and audit of access are standard in open-source and student hospital systems (see section 6). — [GitHub: jmrashed/hospital-management-system](https://github.com/jmrashed/hospital-management-system)

### Inferences
Feature-by-feature verdict (commodity = sourced equivalent found above; "assumed commodity" = widely standard but not sourced in this session):

| MediBridge feature | Verdict | Closest existing equivalents |
|---|---|---|
| Three roles (patient / doctor / admin) | Commodity | Practo + Ray, Eka + Eka Doc, every GitHub HMS |
| Search/filter by specialization, city, mode, rating | Commodity (filters by mode/rating assumed) | Practo, Zocdoc, Doctolib, Bajaj |
| Clinic map | Assumed commodity | Not sourced |
| Slot booking from weekly availability, no double booking | Commodity | Practo, Zocdoc, Doctolib, OpenEMR |
| Cancel / reschedule | Assumed commodity | Not sourced |
| Doctor writes record (diagnosis, vitals, summary, follow-up) | Commodity | Practo Ray, Eka Doc, HealthPlix, OpenMRS/Bahmni/OpenEMR |
| Multi-line prescriptions | Commodity | HealthPlix, Eka Doc, Practo Ray |
| Patient views records and prescriptions | Commodity | ABHA PHR apps, Eka Care, Bajaj, MyChart |
| Medication reminders + intake logging + adherence chart | Commodity | Medisafe, Eka Care, MyChart Care Companion |
| Unified Health Timeline | Commodity in substance | Practo "medical timeline", ABHA-linked record lists |
| Doctor reviews | Commodity | Practo, Zocdoc |
| In-app notifications | Commodity | NHS App, all majors |
| Admin stats, verification, activation, CRUD, reported issues, audit log | Commodity (internal tooling; not public-facing in commercial apps) | GitHub HMS projects, OpenEMR |
| Health-education library | Commodity | MyChart Care Companion; Practo and 1mg publish health articles (assumed) |
| Emergency info page | Assumed commodity | Not sourced |
| Care Score (0-100; adherence 40%, checkup recency 35%, follow-up completion 25%) | No direct equivalent found | Medisafe adherence score (medication only); MyChart health-maintenance flags; Care Companion task check-ins |

- The Care Score is a recombination of existing signals. Medisafe scores adherence, MyChart flags overdue check-ups, Eka/HealthPlix chase follow-ups. I did not find a consumer platform that fuses the three into one patient-visible number, but I searched for this with a single query, so absence of evidence here is weak. Insurer wellness scores and the "Patient Activation Measure" used in research are adjacent concepts that were not checked.
- The defensible differentiation is therefore the post-visit continuity loop (prescription becomes reminders, reminders become adherence, follow-up date becomes a tracked obligation, all of it rolls into one score and timeline). This lines up with the documented gap in ABDM and the marketplaces (section 5), and is a stronger argument than any single feature.
- A candid framing for evaluators: booking, records, prescriptions, reviews and admin are table stakes that already exist; the project's contribution is in closing the loop after the consultation.

### Gaps
- No source confirming or denying that Practo, Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg or MediBuddy show a patient-facing composite engagement/health score. Apollo and insurers run health-risk-score programmes that should be checked before claiming novelty.
- No source on whether any Indian marketplace auto-generates medication reminders directly from the doctor's prescription lines (this is the specific linkage MediBridge would need to be different).
- Clinic map, reschedule, consultation-mode filter and emergency page were not individually sourced.

- No current user figures found for Tata 1mg, MediBuddy (post-2022), Eka Care, HealthPlix, MyChart, Doctor Anywhere, athenaPatient, Healow.
- Zocdoc first-party figures not found.
- The Similarweb URL is the nearest result to the "5 lakh doctors / 50 crore patients" snippet but the snippet-to-URL mapping is uncertain.

## 5. Documented weaknesses, complaints and gaps

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced gaps are (a) review trustworthiness on Practo, by user allegation, and (b) ABDM's failure so far to turn stored records into continuity of care, by a July 2026 policy analysis. The second directly supports a "post-visit continuity" positioning.

### Cited Findings
- Practo reviews, user allegations on Trustpilot: reviewers say genuine negative reviews are not shown while paid or fake 5-star reviews are; one describes a review held 2-4 weeks, published heavily redacted, then removed; others say reviews with supporting documents were not published. These are unverified individual complaints. — [Trustpilot: practo.com](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/practo.com)
- Practo, doctor-side complaints on Trustpilot: doctors allege terms were changed without notice and consultation fees were not paid; reviewers question a review site that is paid by the doctors it rates. — [Trustpilot: practo.com, page 3](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/practo.com?page=3)
- ABDM continuity gap: "The continuity loop that would make ABDM clinically meaningful for chronic disease management remains largely untapped." — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- ABDM record retrieval: "Records are produced at outreach or front-desk events but are not consistently retrieved at subsequent facility visits." — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- ABDM adoption funnel: M1 reached mass scale but "M3, the milestone that would make ABDM genuinely valuable for care coordination, remains extremely low"; the steepest drop is between M2 and M3. — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- ABDM consent friction: "The consent framework emerged as a persistent friction point"; "this loop has proven difficult to close in high-volume settings". — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- Doctor-side burden: "Provider workflow burden is a persistent barrier"; required steps "can add time rather than save it". — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- Legacy integration: interoperability with the installed base of hospital information systems "remains unresolved"; integration cost for mid-sized providers estimated at INR 30-50 lakh. — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)
- Private-sector participation: 44% of registered facilities are private but mostly small to mid-sized and lacking awareness or incentive to link records or issue digital prescriptions; as of October 2024 only about 17,000 of 1.3 lakh ABDM-enabled facilities were private. — [Arthur D. Little / NATHEALTH, 2024](https://nathealthindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CATALYZING-DIGITAL-HEALTH-IN-INDIA.pdf); [OC Academy](https://www.ocacademy.in/blogs/abdm-private-adoption-challenges)
- Fragmentation: data fragmentation makes it hard to access and share records across providers, leading to delays, repeated diagnostics and suboptimal care. — [Arthur D. Little, 2024](https://www.adlittle.com/sites/default/files/reports/ADL_Catalyzing_digital_health_India_2024_0.pdf)
- Sector fragility: MFine cut 50-75% of staff in 2022; Babylon went from a $4.2 billion listing to bankruptcy in about two years after UK regulators questioned governance and patient safety and it lost NHS contracts. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/exclusive-healthtech-startup-mfine-fires-600-employees); [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/digital-health/how-babylon-health-went-from-2b-to-bankrupt/)
- Headline inflation: ABHA counts overstate unique active patients because of CoWIN seeding. — [HMPI, 9 Jul 2026](https://hmpi.org/2026/07/09/from-infrastructure-to-impact-operationalizing-indias-ayushman-bharat-digital-mission/) (fetched)

### Inferences
- Marketplaces are organised around the transaction (booking, consult, pharmacy order); what happens between visits is where the evidence shows the weakest coverage in India. MediBridge's adherence logging, follow-up tracking and Care Score address that interval.
- Doctor-side burden is a real adoption barrier, so any MediBridge feature that asks doctors for extra data entry inherits the same weakness. Deriving reminders and follow-up tasks automatically from what the doctor already writes would be the stronger design.
- Review trust is a credible secondary differentiator only if MediBridge restricts reviews to completed appointments and says so; the Practo evidence is anecdotal user complaints, not an audit.

### Gaps
- No independent study, regulator finding or news investigation on fake reviews at Practo was retrieved; only Trustpilot allegations.
- No sourced data on no-show rates, follow-up completion rates or medication adherence rates among Indian platform users.
- The OC Academy and ADL URLs are the likely carriers of the 17,000 / 1.3 lakh and 44% figures respectively, but the snippet did not map each figure to one URL with certainty.
- No complaints data gathered for Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg, MediBuddy, Zocdoc, Doctolib or Teladoc.

## 6. Open-source and student-project equivalents

### Takeaway
The base MediBridge feature set (three roles, booking, records, prescriptions, admin dashboard) is replicated by mature open-source systems and by many GitHub student projects on the same Node/Express/MySQL stack. None of the repos found advertises an adherence chart or engagement score.

### Cited Findings
- OpenEMR: free, open source; patient demographics, appointment scheduling, in-house pharmacy support, patient portal with secure chat, online payments, encrypted access to patient documents. — [Techjockey: open-source hospital management software](https://www.techjockey.com/blog/open-source-and-free-hospital-management-software/amp)
- Bahmni: open-source EMR and hospital system for low-resource settings, built on OpenMRS, adding diagnostics, pharmacy, radiology, lab, billing and patient registration modules; adopted in over 50 countries. — [Bahmni wiki: About Bahmni](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/x/KIDg); [Synodus](https://synodus.com/blog/healthcare/free-hospital-information-system-open-source/)
- OpenMRS: platform emphasising data interoperability and adding functions without modifying core code; base for Bahmni. — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/blog/open-source-and-free-hospital-management-software/amp)
- HospitalRun: offline-capable; manages inventory, patient information, assessments and procedures, appointments, labs, imaging and medication. — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/five-free-or-open-source-healthcare-software-tools-ready-to-serve-and-help-heal/)
- VRajaSubramanian/Hospital-Management-System (Node.js, HTML, CSS, MongoDB): patient and doctor registration/login, appointment booking, medical record viewing, prescription generation, doctor schedule management. — [GitHub](https://github.com/VRajaSubramanian/Hospital-Management-System)
- ray8118/hospital_management_system (MERN): patients log in, schedule appointments, view prescriptions; admin dashboard for managing doctors. — [ecosyste.ms listing](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fray8118%2Fhospital_management_system)
- Mishra-coder/Appointment-Booking-System (React, Node, Express, Prisma, MySQL): patients book, doctors manage schedules, administrators oversee the platform. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Mishra-coder/Appointment-Booking-System)
- jmrashed/hospital-management-system (Node, Express, MySQL, MongoDB, React): multiple user roles with different access levels. — [GitHub](https://github.com/jmrashed/hospital-management-system)
- tom474/hospital_management_website (React, Node, Express, MySQL, MongoDB): patient management, staff scheduling, appointments, medical records. — [ecosyste.ms listing](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Ftom474%2Fhospital_management_website)
- Further examples: HackRoot1/HMS, sushantkr961/HospitalManagement, and the GitHub "clinic" topic. — [GitHub: HackRoot1/HMS](https://github.com/HackRoot1/HMS); [GitHub topic: clinic](https://www.github.com/topics/clinic)

### Inferences
- The evaluators' objection applies twice: commercially (Practo and peers) and academically ("hospital management system with patient/doctor/admin" is among the most common full-stack student projects). The core CRUD cannot carry the novelty claim.
- From the descriptions retrieved, the student repos stop at booking + records + prescriptions + admin. Medication intake logging, an adherence chart, a composite score, a unified timeline and an audit log were not mentioned in any of them, so that layer is what separates MediBridge from the typical project. This rests on README-level summaries of about seven repos, not a systematic survey.

### Gaps
- Repos were not opened or inspected; star counts, activity and actual code coverage are unknown.
- HospitalRun's maintenance status in 2026 was not checked.
- No search was run specifically for student or open-source projects with adherence tracking or patient engagement scores, so their absence is not established.
