The problem

Care breaks at the handoffs, not during the visit.

Each step works in isolation. Between the steps, information is re-typed, re-asked or simply lost, and the patient is the one left waiting.

01 · Before & after a visit

The doctor starts from zero, the patient forgets the plan

Consults are short. Time goes on re-asking history, allergies and current medicines. Afterwards, instructions live in the patient's memory and nobody checks whether the follow-up test happened.

Smart Brief before the visit, Visit Loop after it.
02 · Leaving the hospital

Medically fit at noon, still in the bed at five

Billing, insurance, pharmacy, nursing and records each run their own process. Nobody can see which one is holding the patient back, so the bed stays blocked and the family keeps asking.

Discharge Flow names the department causing the delay.
03 · Between hospitals

Finding a bed one phone call at a time

When a hospital can't provide the care a patient needs, staff phone around, read the case out over the line, and wait. Minutes matter most in exactly these cases.

TransferConnect sends one request to the best-matched hospitals.

How MediBridge closes them

Four connected modules, one patient record underneath.

Everything the patient or a clinician enters once is reused at every later step, so nobody types it twice.
