Recognizing when to seek emergency care

These are general warning signs, not a diagnostic checklist. When in doubt, seek care immediately rather than waiting.

❌ Seek emergency care for

  • Severe chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Sudden weakness, numbness or slurred speech
  • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • Loss of consciousness or severe confusion
  • Severe allergic reaction (swelling, trouble breathing)
  • Suspected poisoning or overdose

📞 Before help arrives

  • Call your local emergency number right away
  • Stay calm and keep the person still and comfortable
  • Note known allergies, medications and conditions
  • Do not give food, water or medication unless instructed
  • Keep your home first-aid kit and emergency contacts handy

Home first-aid kit checklist

Adhesive bandages • antiseptic wipes • sterile gauze • adhesive tape • digital thermometer • disposable gloves • any personal emergency medication. Review and restock every 6 months.
