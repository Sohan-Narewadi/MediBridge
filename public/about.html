About

MediBridge is built for the moments between appointments.

Booking a doctor, storing a prescription and sending a reminder are solved problems, and plenty of products do them well. MediBridge keeps those foundations, but its focus is the handoff: the point where responsibility for a patient moves from one person, department or hospital to the next. That's where information gets re-typed or lost, and where patients wait without knowing why.

Three design principles

Enter once

A patient's biodata, allergies, medicines and pre-visit answers are captured once and reused in the doctor's brief, the discharge desk and the transfer summary.

Name the bottleneck

Every handoff has an owner and a timer, so a delay points to a department and a reason, not to "the hospital" in general.

Close the loop

Each visit ends with commitments: a test, a follow-up, a habit. All three roles can see which ones are still open.

Sustainable Development Goals

03Primary

Good Health & Well-being

Continuity of care is a health outcome in its own right. MediBridge supports it by:

  • letting doctors spend consult time on care instead of re-taking history
  • keeping follow-ups and medication adherence visible after each visit
  • getting critical patients to a capable hospital faster
09Supporting

Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Shared digital infrastructure between departments and between hospitals replaces phone calls and paper handoffs. A normalised relational schema, a documented REST API and role-based access make it auditable.

10Supporting

Reduced Inequalities

Patients without an advocate in the hospital usually wait longest and understand least. Transparent discharge status, plain-language plans and fee visibility narrow that gap.

Important note

MediBridge is an academic full-stack project. Every doctor, patient, hospital and medical record shown is fictional demo data. It is not a certified medical-records system and must not be used for real clinical decisions.
