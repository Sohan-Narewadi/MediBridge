About MediBridge

MediBridge is a smart healthcare access & management platform connecting patients, doctors and administrators on one system — appointment booking with real-time availability, structured medical records and prescriptions, medication reminders with adherence tracking, and a personal Care Score that summarizes healthcare engagement in one number.

Our approach to the Sustainable Development Goals

SDG 3 — Good Health & Well-being (primary)

By making it easier to find care, keep appointments, track medications and understand one's own health history, MediBridge supports consistent engagement with preventive and ongoing care.

SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

A real relational database, REST API and role-based web application demonstrate how accessible digital infrastructure can modernize healthcare delivery.

SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

Transparent fees, video consultation options and a searchable directory reduce friction and information gaps that disproportionately affect underserved patients.

Important note

MediBridge was built as an academic full-stack development project. All doctors, patients, and medical data shown are entirely fictional demo data. It is not a certified medical records system and should not be used for real clinical decisions.
