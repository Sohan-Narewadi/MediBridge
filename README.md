# MediBridge
Smart Healthcare Access &amp; Management Platform
MediBridge is a full-stack digital healthcare platform designed to make healthcare services more accessible, organized, and user-friendly. The platform connects patients, doctors, and healthcare administrators through a centralized system where users can discover healthcare providers, book appointments, manage medical records, receive health-related reminders, and access essential healthcare information.

The system will provide separate dashboards for patients, doctors, and administrators, enabling secure role-based access and efficient management of healthcare services. Patients can maintain their profiles and medical history, schedule appointments, track upcoming consultations, and manage their healthcare activities. Doctors can manage appointments and patient information, while administrators can oversee users, doctors, appointments, and platform activity.

The project aims to use technology to improve access to healthcare, particularly for people who may face difficulties navigating traditional healthcare services. By providing a centralized and accessible digital platform, MediBridge contributes primarily to SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being, while also supporting SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure and SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities.
